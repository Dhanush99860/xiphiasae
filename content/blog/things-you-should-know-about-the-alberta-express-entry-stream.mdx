If selected, you receive an invitation through your Express Entry portal. Invited applicants are given a specific number of days to apply for a provincial nomination to AINP. In case you don't meet the deadline, your invitation gets canceled, according to CIC News.

#### You may receive an invitation (Notification of Interest) if:

- Your profile is active with theÃ‚Â Federal Express EntryÃ‚Â Pool.

- You have expressed interest in permanently settling down in Alberta.

- You have work expertise in an occupation that is of economic importance in Alberta.

- Your Comprehensive Ranking System score is a minimum of 300.

#### Your chances of getting invited increase if:

- You have work expertise in Alberta or an offer of employment from Alberta.

- You have completed your studies at an academic institute in Canada.

- You have a blood relative, parent, child, or sibling living in Alberta.

#### You may not receive an invitation if:

- The expiry of your Express Entry profile is in 3 months or less

- You work in any ineligible occupation.
