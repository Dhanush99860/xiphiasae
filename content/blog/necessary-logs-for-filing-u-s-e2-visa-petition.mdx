**Necessary Logs for filing E2 Visa Petition**

E2 treaty investor visa is a non-immigrant dual intent visa for an Entrepreneur/Businessman to own a new business or manage an existing one. In addition, an applicant can work under US employers in the United States. Its basic requirements are a substantial investment in bone fide enterprises and applicant nationality to come in treaty countries. E2 visa holder dependents & relatives can accompany them. They can also work anywhere in the USA with a valid EAD. The USA E2 visa holder can gain a lot of benefits such as staying for 5 years initially & doing their own choice business without a Green Card/Citizenship, a bright future for their children, and so on.
