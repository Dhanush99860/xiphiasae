### **Why Travel Freedom for Americans Is Changing**

In 2025, the **U.S. passport** dropped to 12th place on the Henley Passport Index, its lowest ranking in two decades. **U.S. citizens** can now access 180 destinations **visa-free**, a decline from previous years. This shift comes from several factors:

- **Reinstated visa requirements:** Countries like Brazil, Myanmar, and Papua New Guinea have brought back visa requirements for **U.S. travelers**, affecting their global mobility.

- **Declining visa-free agreements:** Some nations are creating new reciprocal visa-free agreements with other countries faster than with Americans, which slightly reduces the **U.S. passport**’s strength.

- **Geopolitical shifts:** Changes in global politics and diplomacy have led to stricter entry policies, further limiting **visa-free travel** for **U.S. citizens**.
